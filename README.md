## 🎯 Project Overview
Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by subscription-based and service-oriented businesses. Retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new customers, making churn prediction an important business problem.

This project focuses on predicting customer churn using Machine Learning techniques and customer behavioral analysis. The project was developed for LoyaltyVision Analytics to help businesses identify customers who are likely to discontinue services and enable proactive retention strategies.

## Company Profile
LoyaltyVision Analytics is a customer experience and retention-focused firm specializing in providing actionable insights to subscription-based and service-oriented businesses. The company uses machine learning, behavioral analytics, and personalized marketing strategies to improve customer retention, reduce churn, and enhance service experiences.

## Problem Statement
The company faces a rising churn rate, which impacts both short-term revenue and long-term business growth. Customers discontinue services unexpectedly, making it difficult to maintain profitability and customer loyalty.

The objective of this project is to develop a predictive model that identifies customers likely to churn using factors such as:

- Usage patterns
- Payment history
- Service interactions
- Customer behavior

The insights generated from the model can help businesses deploy targeted customer retention strategies.
